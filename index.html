Current Stuff I'm Working on.

Crafting Dead

Currently, I am working on one of Pit Icarus's modpacks, Crafting Dead Resurrection, a modpack which aims to add all of the roleplay episodes of PopularMMOS' Crafting Dead series inside of an included one map. Currently, I am rewroking the main Crafting Dead mod to be available on 1.5.2, 1.6.x(and hopefully 1.7.x). I aim to rework the assets and code to be better than the original, while being true and authetic.

I also intend to remove as much bugs which exited in the mods, to provide a more stable modpack.

Eviler Minecraft

Currently, I'm working on Eviler Minecraft, A modpack which aims to expand upon on of PopularMMOS' earliest modpacks. It will add many brand new mods, all of which aim to make Minecraft harder.... in a 'Minecraft style'

Like, you know those 'hard' modpacks such as "Blood N' Bones" that make Minecraft hard in a way Minecraft would never ever do, or add mechanics that make Minecraft 'hard' in a 'realistic way'? Like for instance, "RL Craft", which adds "Lycanites Mobs", which has mechanics and models which don't even rometely fit Minecraft.

Yeah, that's not gonna be in there. When you play it, it will not be 'semi realistic'. The modpack is hard in a way without using 'realism' as an excuse for its mechanics. It will also have mods, which will show and tell that it is modded, but not in an extreme way. It tries to be low key. I hope that explanation was good.
